# Deadlines Watch

## Goal
Track funding deadlines, conference CFPs, and workshop dates.

## Sources
- NSF SBE programs (weekly)
- Russell Sage Foundation (weekly)
- Sloan Foundation (weekly)
- Major conference CFPs: NEUDC, PacDev, BREAD, AEA, UEA (weekly)

## Alerts
- 60 days before: first notice
- 30 days before: reminder
- 7 days before: urgent
