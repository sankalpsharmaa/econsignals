# Social Watch

## Goal
Monitor economics discussion on social media, linking posts back to papers.

## Sources
- Bluesky: #EconSky hashtag, tracked author handles (3x daily)
- Mastodon: econtwitter.net (3x daily)
- Twitter/X bridge: Nitter/RSS (best-effort)

## Filters
- Posts from tracked authors
- Posts containing DOIs or paper links
- Posts with #EconTwitter, #EconSky, #DevEcon hashtags
