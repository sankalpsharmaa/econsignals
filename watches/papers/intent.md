# Papers Watch

## Goal
Discover new economics research papers relevant to applied micro, development, and urban economics, with emphasis on India.

## Sources
- OpenAlex (primary)
- Crossref (journal TOC)
- IZA discussion papers
- BREAD working papers
- IMF working papers

## Filters
- JEL codes from profile
- India-related content boost
- Minimum relevance threshold: 0.3

## Output
Feed into newsletter and daily_brief. State in `watches/papers/state.json`.
