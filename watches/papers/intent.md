# Papers Watch

## Goal
Discover new economics research papers relevant to applied micro, development, and urban economics, with emphasis on India.

## Sources
- OpenAlex (primary, 2x daily)
- NBER working papers (daily)
- SSRN econ networks (daily)
- Crossref journal TOC alerts (daily)
- RePEC NEP digests (daily)
- World Bank working papers (daily)
- RSS feeds: VoxDev, VoxEU, NBER Digest, econ blogs (2x daily)

## Filters
- JEL codes from profile
- Tracked author publications
- India-related content boost
- Minimum relevance threshold: 0.3

## Output
Feed into daily_brief lens. Flag high-relevance papers (>0.7) for deep analysis.
