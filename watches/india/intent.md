# India Watch

## Goal
Monitor India-specific economics research, policy papers, and data releases.

## Sources
- IGC publications (weekly)
- India think tanks: NCAER, CPR, ICRIER, IGIDR, APU, IIHS (weekly)
- RBI publications and working papers (weekly)
- MoSPI / NSSO data releases (weekly)
- J-PAL South Asia (weekly)
- OpenAlex with India filter (daily)

## Filters
- Papers mentioning India, Indian states/cities, Indian institutions
- Authors at Indian institutions
- Topics: urbanization, land, housing, migration, labor, infrastructure, governance

## Output
Separate section in daily brief. Cross-reference with papers watch to avoid duplication.
